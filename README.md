**AI-powered real-time insurance platform for autonomous vehicles.**

Autonomous Insurance AI is an intelligent insurance platform designed for autonomous vehicles. Instead of relying only on fixed factors such as vehicle model, driver age, and previous accidents, our system analyzes **real-time driving conditions and vehicle events** to estimate risk dynamically.

## 💡 Problem

Traditional car insurance mainly depends on static information:

* Vehicle model
* Driver profile
* Previous accident history
* Fixed insurance plans

However, autonomous vehicles operate in constantly changing environments. Road conditions, traffic, weather, vehicle behavior, and sudden incidents can change the risk level within seconds.

## Our Solution

Autonomous Insurance AI provides **real-time risk analysis** for autonomous vehicles.

The platform can:

* 📊 Monitor vehicle and environmental data
* 🤖 Analyze risk using AI
* ⚠️ Detect potentially dangerous situations
* 🚗 Track autonomous vehicles in real time
* 📍 Display incidents on an interactive map
* 📈 Calculate a dynamic risk score
* 🛡️ Support dynamic insurance pricing

## 🎯 Key Features

### Real-Time Vehicle Monitoring

The system displays autonomous vehicles and their current status on an interactive map.

### AI Risk Analysis

Vehicle and environmental information is analyzed to estimate the current risk level.

### Incident Detection

When an accident or dangerous event occurs, the system detects the incident and displays relevant information.

### Dynamic Risk Score

Instead of using only a fixed insurance price, the platform calculates a risk score based on current conditions.

### Interactive Dashboard

Insurance companies can monitor their autonomous vehicle fleet and detected incidents through a centralized dashboard.
